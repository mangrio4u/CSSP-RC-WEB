RC Sindhri Participates In

MANZIL

Youth Leadership  •  Livelihoods  •  Entrepreneurship  •  Climate Action  •  Community Development

MANZIL is a youth-focused program in which RC Sindhri participated, focusing on youth leadership, livelihood opportunities, entrepreneurship, employment, climate action and community development. RC Sindhri's MANZIL activities brought together young people from 8 youth groups in UC Sindhri and surrounding communities.

About

MANZIL at RC Sindhri

RC Sindhri participated in MANZIL activities encompassing youth leadership development, livelihood and entrepreneurship programs, climate change awareness and action, employment support, community engagement and organization/documentation activities.

Through MANZIL, young people from RC Sindhri's 8 youth groups worked toward building their capacities, establishing community structures and engaging in meaningful development work across UC Sindhri and surrounding communities.

Participation

Youth Groups Engaged in MANZIL

8 Youth Groups
200 Youth Members

RC Sindhri's 8 youth groups (200 total members — 25 members per group, including 2 girls' groups) were engaged in community initiatives. Representatives from the groups participated in training, events and community work across UC Sindhri and its surrounding communities.

⚠️
Participation Clarification: Not all 200 members attended every activity. Typically, one or two representatives from each group participated in particular activities, events and training sessions on behalf of their group.
