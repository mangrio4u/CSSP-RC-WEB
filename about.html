Who We Are

Rozgar Center Sindhri

RC Sindhri (Rozgar Center Sindhri) serves as a platform for youth skills development, livelihood opportunities, entrepreneurship, leadership, awareness and community development in Village Haji Hamer Mangrio, UC Sindhri, Taluka Sindhri, District Mirpur Khas, Sindh.

The center works with young people and surrounding communities through vocational training, youth groups, leadership development, community awareness sessions, health outreach, climate action activities, GBV awareness and cultural programs.

RC Sindhri's work has included activities connected with PTY (Power to Youth) and MANZIL as well as independent community outreach and skills initiatives.

Development Journey

RC Sindhri's Story

How RC Sindhri has grown — from its earliest activities through to broader community programs.

P1

Phase 1

Power to Youth (PTY)

RC Sindhri emerged in connection with PTY (Power to Youth) activities. PTY is a youth-focused project associated with CSSP and its partners, with NCA Pakistan as lead partner, supported by EU funding. RC Sindhri participated in PTY activities including vocational training, youth development and community awareness.

🟢 Officially documented — CSSP/NCA PTY materials
P2

Phase 2

Skills & Livelihood Development

Vocational and technical training was conducted across 7 trades: E-Commerce, Beautician, Dress Making, Solar, Mobile Repairing, Embroidery and Masonry. A total of 240 trainees participated across training years 2023 and 2025.

🔵 RC Sindhri-provided information
P3

Phase 3

Youth Groups

RC Sindhri established 8 youth groups with 175+ youth members. Representatives from these groups participate in youth, community, climate, GBV, education and development activities.

⚠️ The youth groups currently do not have separate official names. Not all 175+ members attended every activity.
🔵 RC Sindhri-provided information
P4

Phase 4

MANZIL

RC Sindhri participated in MANZIL activities including youth leadership, livelihoods, entrepreneurship, employment, climate action, community engagement and organization/documentation. A separate MANZIL body/structure was formed.

🔵 RC Sindhri-provided information
P5

Phase 5

Community Outreach

RC Sindhri expanded into community outreach activities including health awareness, vaccination mobilization (COVID-19, MR, Polio), NADRA MRV facilitation, awareness sessions and community dialogue.

🔵 RC Sindhri-provided information
