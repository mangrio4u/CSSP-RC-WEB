Source Verification Classification System

To maintain maximum transparency and development-sector standards, all data and reports presented on this website are categorized under three clear verification levels:

🟢 Officially Documented

Verified through official external partner publications, public CSSP newsletters (e.g. Dec 2023 EU/NCA PTY newsletter), or public donor documentations.

🔵 RC Sindhri-Provided Info

Specific local figures (e.g. 240 vocational trainees across 7 trades, 8 youth groups, 175+ members, ~300 NICs) reported by RC Sindhri team.

🟡 To Be Verified

Contextual details, precise establishment dates, or NGO registration counts reserved pending official document releases.
