Project Background & Context

🟢 Officially Documented Source

Power to Youth (PTY) is a youth-focused project associated with CSSP (Civil Society Support Programme) and its partners. CSSP's official newsletter material documents PTY activities including youth empowerment, vocational training and youth participation in Mirpur Khas district. CSSP's December 2023 newsletter describes PTY as an EU-funded project with NCA Pakistan as lead partner.

Verification Note: PTY is an officially documented program context. RC Sindhri participated in PTY activities in UC Sindhri, establishing local vocational training and youth engagement.

RC Sindhri has formed 8 youth groups with 200 total members — 25 members per group, including 2 girls' groups. Representatives from these groups actively participate in various youth leadership, community development, climate change awareness, GBV awareness, education, and cultural activities across UC Sindhri.

Participation Clarification: One or two representatives from each youth group typically participate in specific trainings, sessions, or community activities on behalf of their group, rather than all 200 members attending every individual session.

NADRA Mobile Registration Van (MRV) Facilitation

Facilitating Civil Registration & National Identity Cards (NIC)

🔵 RC Sindhri-Provided Data

To assist rural residents—especially women, youth, and elderly citizens who face travel hurdles to urban centers—RC Sindhri mobilized the community and coordinated a 2-day visit by the NADRA Mobile Registration Van (MRV).

400
Total Tokens Issued
200 tokens/day across 2 days
160
NIC Cards Processed
Successfully processed/issued
2
Days of Operation
Across two central village venues

Day 1 Venue

Otaq Waheed Mangrio: Initial community registration camp serving surrounding hamlets.

Day 2 Venue

RC Sindhri, Village Haji Hameer Mangrio: Second registration day accommodating wide community turnout.

Some applications were rejected due to documentation issues; 8 cases involved expired documents/cards that required renewal follow-up.
Service Delivery Note: NADRA delivered the registration service; RC Sindhri facilitated community mobilization and access. 400 tokens were issued/processed through the two-day facilitation activity, with 160 NIC cards successfully processed/issued.
