Project Background & Context

🟢 Officially Documented Source

Power to Youth (PTY) is a youth-focused project associated with CSSP (Civil Society Support Programme) and its partners. CSSP's official newsletter material documents PTY activities including youth empowerment, vocational training and youth participation in Mirpur Khas district. CSSP's December 2023 newsletter describes PTY as an EU-funded project with NCA Pakistan as lead partner.

Verification Note: PTY is an officially documented program context. RC Sindhri participated in PTY activities in UC Sindhri, establishing local vocational training and youth engagement.

RC Sindhri Youth Groups

8
Active Youth Groups
175+
Total Youth Members

RC Sindhri has engaged eight youth groups with more than 175 young members. Representatives from these groups actively participate in various youth leadership, community development, climate change awareness, GBV awareness, education, and cultural activities across UC Sindhri.

Participation Clarification: One or two representatives from each youth group typically participate in specific trainings, sessions, or community activities on behalf of their group, rather than all 175+ members attending every individual session.

PTY Core Activity Streams

Comprehensive activities conducted under PTY at RC Sindhri

Vocational Training

Technical training across 7 confirmed trades for 240 youth trainees.

Regular group meetings for 8 youth groups to discuss community development.
