About Us

RC Sindhri — Rozgar Center Sindhri

RC Sindhri (Rozgar Center Sindhri) serves as a platform for youth skills development, livelihood opportunities, entrepreneurship, leadership, awareness and community development in Village Haji Hamer Mangrio, UC Sindhri, Taluka Sindhri, District Mirpur Khas, Sindh.

The center's work has included activities connected with the PTY (Power to Youth) and MANZIL projects, as well as independent community outreach and skills initiatives serving young people and communities across the union council.

Through structured vocational training, youth group formation, awareness sessions, and community engagement, RC Sindhri bridges the gap between youth potential and real community impact.

Our Journey

RC Sindhri's Development Journey

From community engagement to structured youth development and outreach

1

Phase 1 · Power to Youth (PTY)

Power to Youth (PTY)

RC Sindhri emerged in connection with PTY activities — a program designed to empower young people through vocational skills, livelihood support, and entrepreneurship. This phase laid the foundation for structured youth development activities at the center.

2

Phase 2 · Skills & Livelihood Development

Skills & Livelihood Development

Vocational and technical training was delivered across multiple trades — including e-commerce, solar, mobile repairing, dress making, masonry, embroidery and beautician services — reaching 240+ trainees in training years 2023 and 2025.

3

Phase 3 · Youth Group Formation

Youth Groups

8 structured youth groups were established involving 175+ young people across UC Sindhri, creating a network for peer engagement, leadership development, community activities, and collective action within the union council.

4

Phase 4 · MANZIL

MANZIL Project

The MANZIL project expanded RC Sindhri's scope to include youth leadership, livelihood and entrepreneurship support, employment facilitation, climate action awareness, community engagement, and systematic documentation of community-level activities.

5

Phase 5 · Community Outreach

Community Outreach

A dedicated outreach program covering health awareness, vaccination mobilization, NADRA MRV facilitation (approx. 300 beneficiaries), GBV awareness, 10+ stage drama performances, 20+ awareness sessions, and community dialogue across surrounding villages.
