Gender-Based Violence — GBV Awareness

Promoting Social Protection, Protection & Community Respect

🔵 RC Sindhri-Provided

RC Sindhri conducts sensitive and respectful GBV awareness sessions to promote protection, gender rights, and community harmony among women, youth, and families in UC Sindhri.

GBV Awareness Sessions

Community workshops focusing on rights, social protection, and respect.

Women & Girls Sessions

Targeted awareness for women and young girls regarding community safety and empowerment.

Awareness Walks

Community walks organized to promote message of non-violence and equality.

Theatre & Drama

Stage drama performances on social themes, early marriage, and family protection.

Privacy & Respect Commitment: All GBV awareness activities follow strict ethical standards. No private or sensitive survivor information is shared or published.

Health Awareness & Vaccination Mobilization

Community Mobilization in Support of Public Health Authorities

🔵 RC Sindhri-Provided

RC Sindhri worked with health workers to support awareness and vaccination mobilization activities across surrounding communities in UC Sindhri.

COVID-19 Awareness

Community awareness and mobilization for COVID-19 vaccination drives.

Measles-Rubella vaccination awareness and mobilization support.

Polio Vaccination

Health Awareness

General health awareness sessions for families and local youth across surrounding villages.

Role Clarification: RC Sindhri's role was community awareness, mobilization, and facilitation. Health workers/health authorities administered vaccination services. RC Sindhri is NOT a healthcare provider.

NADRA Mobile Registration Van (MRV) Facilitation

Assisting Residents Access National Identity Services

🔵 RC Sindhri-Provided

RC Sindhri facilitated a two-day NADRA Mobile Registration Van (MRV) visit, assisting residents across surrounding villages to access National Identity Card (NIC) registration services.

Tokens Issued

Some applications were rejected due to documentation issues. 8 cases involved expired documents/cards.
Service Delivery Note: NADRA delivered the registration service. RC Sindhri facilitated community mobilization and access. 400 tokens were issued/processed through the two-day facilitation activity, with 160 NIC cards successfully processed/issued.
