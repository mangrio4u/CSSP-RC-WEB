Core Programs & Streams

RC Sindhri Programs

From Youth Potential to Community Impact — Discover our structured initiatives in skills training, youth leadership, education, climate action and community development across UC Sindhri.

All Active Program Streams

Verified community programs serving 18 villages across UC Sindhri

Vocational Development

Skills & Vocational Training

240 youth trainees across 7 verified trades: E-Commerce (15), Beautician (25), Dress Making (50), Solar Repairing (50), Mobile Repairing (25), Embroidery (25), and Masonry (50).

240 Trainees • 15+ Livelihoods • 15+ Enterprises
View Skills Dashboard
Youth Leadership

Youth Groups & Leadership

8 organized youth groups comprising 200 total members (25 members per group, including 2 girls' groups). Group representatives actively engage in community dialogue and development campaigns.

8 Youth Groups • 200 Members • 2 Girls' Groups
View Youth Groups
Education & Advocacy

Education Advocacy & Support

Community advocacy opened GBPS Ahmed Abupoto in Khani Mangrio (50+ students) and facilitated the relocation of Girls' College Din Mohammed Junejo to the main road (80+ girls).

GBPS School • Girls' College • Institutional Liaison
View Education Initiatives
Environment & Resilience

Climate Action & Tree Plantation

3-day Climate Change Training for 100 youth across 4 groups (Bisma Hotel & Press Club, Mirpur Khas). Followed by village-to-village climate campaigns and tree plantation drives.

100 Youth Trained • 4 Groups • Tree Plantation
View Climate Action
Community Theatre

Stage Drama & Social Awareness

10 stage drama performances by an 8-member youth theatre troupe covering 11 critical community themes: climate change, GBV, child rights, education, early marriage, and folk culture.

10 Performances • 11 Social Themes • 8 Actors
View Stage Drama
Social Protection

GBV & Gender Rights Awareness

Community dialogue, sensitization campaigns, and workshops addressing gender rights, women and girls' safety, social protection, and early child marriage prevention.

Sensitization • Early Marriage Prevention • Women Safety
View GBV Section
Public Health Mobilization

Health Awareness & Mobilization

Supporting health workers through mobilization: COVID-19 (200+ reached), MR Measles & Rubella (300+ reached), and resolving 60+ Polio refusal cases. Vaccinations administered by health authorities.

COVID-19 • Measles-Rubella • Polio Refusal Dialogue
View Health Outreach
Civil Registration

NADRA Mobile Van Facilitation

400 tokens issued across a 2-day mobile unit camp (Day 1: Otaq Waheed Mangrio, Day 2: RC Sindhri Haji Hamer Mangrio), resulting in 160 NIC cards successfully processed by NADRA.

400 Tokens • 160 NIC Cards Issued • 2 Days
View NADRA Details
Community Forum

Mach Kachehri Dialogues

Traditional communal evening assemblies bringing youth and village elders together around the fire to discuss local issues, climate impacts, livelihoods, and civic solutions.

Traditional Problem Solving • Youth & Elders
View Mach Kachehri
