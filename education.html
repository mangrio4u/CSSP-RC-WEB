Education Advocacy & Community Impact

Community Mobilization & Institutional Follow-up for Local Schools & Colleges

RC Sindhri-provided data

Through active youth groups, community dialogues, and engagement with district education authorities, community advocacy and engagement contributed to significant educational improvements across UC Sindhri:

50+
Enrolled Students

GBPS Ahmed Abupoto

Located in Village Khani Mangrio. The school was opened/arranged through sustained community advocacy and mobilization, currently serving over 50+ local children.

80+
Enrolled Girls

Girls' College — Din Mohammed Junejo

Successfully shifted from an inconvenient location near the Din Mohammed Junejo stop/shop-hotel area to a safer, more accessible location on the main road, now serving 80+ girls.

Campus Relocation

Girls' Higher School

The girls' higher school was also shifted to a better accessible location, improving learning facilities, student safety, and daily attendance for local female students.

Advocacy Note: Community advocacy and engagement contributed to these educational milestones. RC Sindhri facilitated community mobilization and advocacy alongside education department stakeholders.

Government & Institutional Engagement

Liaison with Education Officers, Civil Administration & Grievance Redressal

RC Sindhri-provided data

To ensure sustained institutional responsiveness for rural schools and community needs, youth groups and community elders maintained structured engagement with official educational administrators:

Amir Bux Panhwar

TEO Primary

Dialogue regarding primary school staff availability, local enrollment, and village schooling needs.

Mustafa Jamal

TEO Higher Secondary

Coordination regarding girls' college access, campus shifting to the main road, and secondary schooling facilities.

Nabi Bux Gurgez

DEO Primary

District-level representation regarding union council school openings and local community petitions.

Civil Administration Follow-Up

Meetings and petitions with Assistant Commissioner (AC) and Deputy Commissioner (DC) authorities in Mirpur Khas regarding community and schooling development.

Pakistan Citizens Portal

Structured escalation and tracking of local educational and civic concerns through the Pakistan Citizens Portal for institutional accountability and public resolution.
